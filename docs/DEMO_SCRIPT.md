# Demo video script (2:45)

Judging weighs presentation equally with the other four criteria, and Best Demo Delivery is its own $5,000 prize. Record at 1440 wide, browser zoom 90%, sandbox mode on, Claude key set. Reset demo data before each take: `POST /demo/reset`.

## 0:00 to 0:15. The problem

**Visual:** Footage from the Vermont and Maine trip. A group chat on a phone: "who's actually in?" with no replies.

**Voice:** "Every group trip dies the same way. Someone floats a plan, four people say maybe, and the one who books the van ends up chasing everyone for money."

## 0:15 to 0:30. The idea

**Visual:** Home page. The departure board flips in.

**Voice:** "Sidequest is a group trip that only runs if enough people commit. You hold your spot with PayPal. Nobody pays unless it happens, and every extra person makes it cheaper for everyone."

## 0:30 to 1:05. The hero moment

**Visual:** Open the invite link for Apple picking, the page a friend would get. Point at the faces, 1 seat to go, and "Up to $81.00". Click Hold, approve on PayPal's sandbox page. The full quest page opens: the board flips to IT'S ON, the stamp lands, the money route moves.

**Voice:** "That's a real PayPal authorization at the max price. Seat five just filled, so the quest is on. Two more people join..." (Demo controls, add 2) "...and everyone's share drops from $81 to $67.29."

## 1:05 to 1:25. Lock and charge

**Visual:** Switch to Hon. Lock and charge. Money route reaches Everyone charged. Scroll the money log.

**Voice:** "When the host locks, PayPal captures each hold at the final split. Never more than anyone approved. Every call is in the log with its PayPal ID, and webhooks confirm each one."

## 1:25 to 2:05. The agent

**Visual:** Breakneck Ridge. As Dev, type "I'm sick, I can't make it Saturday." The agent replies. Switch to Ana. The proposal card lists the exact PayPal operations. Approve.

**Voice:** "The agent runs the group chat. Dev drops out after everyone's been charged. The agent can't move money by itself, so it proposes a swap: capture Leo's standby hold, refund Dev. The host sees exactly what will run on PayPal and approves it in one tap."

## 2:05 to 2:25. Settle up and payout

**Visual:** Settle up with a higher total. Sidequest drafts the invoices. Approve. Trip done, payout.

**Voice:** "Gas came in over. Sidequest bills everyone their share with PayPal invoices through the Agent Toolkit. After the trip, the host gets paid through PayPal Payouts."

## 2:25 to 2:40. Agentic commerce

**Visual:** Claude Desktop with Sidequest connected. "Find me something cheap to do Saturday." Claude lists quests and returns a PayPal approval link.

**Voice:** "Sidequest is also an MCP server, so your own AI assistant can find a quest and hold your spot. You still approve every hold on PayPal."

## 2:40 to 2:45. Close

**Visual:** Home page board: NOW BOARDING.

**Voice:** "Sidequest. Plans that happen."
